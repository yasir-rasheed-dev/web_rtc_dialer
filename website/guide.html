Onboarding

Getting Started

ringNex is multi-tenant — every login belongs to a workspace, and every record you see is scoped to that workspace.

  1. Open ringNex in your browser (or the desktop app). Enter your workspace, email and password.
  2. If two-factor authentication is enabled on your account, enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app. First-time enrolment shows a QR code to scan.
  3. On sign-in your presence is set to Ready automatically, so calls can route to you straight away. A new login instantly ends any older session for the same account.
  4. Grant microphone access when the browser asks. Without it, calls cannot connect.
  5. The sidebar only shows the pages your role allows — if you don't see a feature described in this guide, your role hasn't been granted it.
Keep your status accurate. Switch to Paused or Offline during breaks so customer calls aren't routed to an unavailable agent. Your status also flips to On-Call automatically while you're on a call and back to Ready when it ends.
Core Workflow

The Softphone

A real SIP phone in the browser — one registration per session. Every call starts here.

Manual dial

Type the number into the field and press the green call button. Use the on-screen keypad or your keyboard.

Dial from a record

Start a call straight from a Contact, a Lead, or any row in Call Logs — the number populates automatically.

Incoming calls

A call overlay appears for every inbound call with Answer and Decline. The caller ID is matched against your saved contacts.

Missed calls

If your role has voicemail enabled, an unanswered call falls through to voicemail. Otherwise the caller hears a busy tone and the call is logged as missed.

Outbound caller ID. Your outbound calls present the DID assigned to your agent account. If no DID is assigned, your workspace's default number is used.
Active Call

In-Call Controls

The call overlay surfaces these controls during any live call. In the desktop app you can pop the overlay out into a detachable, always-on-top window mid-call.

ControlFunction
Mute
Disables your outgoing audio. The customer can't hear you while muted; you still hear them.
Hold / Resume
Places the customer on hold with hold music. You're free to speak or check notes. Resume to reconnect instantly. Requires the Hold Call permission.
Keypad (DTMF)
Sends touch-tones for IVR navigation during the call.
Transfer
Blind or warm/attended transfer to another agent in your workspace. See the Transfers section.
Add participant
Pulls another agent or an external number into a multi-party conference bridge. Requires the Add Participant permission.
Save as lead
Creates or updates a Lead record for the current caller without leaving the call.
Hang up
Ends the call. If the call was connected, the End-Call popup opens for wrap-up.
Hold behaviour: only the customer is held. You hear silence and can talk freely; the customer hears music until you resume.
Wrap-up

End-Call Popup & Dispositions

When a connected call ends, the End-Call popup opens automatically so nothing is lost (role-gated — an admin can turn it off).

Prefilled details

Number and call duration are filled in for you. Edit the client's name and details, and toggle save to contact to keep them.

Disposition

Pick a disposition from your workspace's shared list (each has a name and colour). The same list is used in the softphone, the auto-dialer and the logs.

Next follow-up

Set a follow-up date and time. It appears on the Follow-ups dashboard and adds to the due badge.

Remarks, tags & files

Remarks are required. Add tags for filtering later, and attach files (screenshots, documents) to the call/lead.

Everything captured here is written to the call log and, where relevant, mirrored onto the Lead record so the customer's history stays in one place.
Call Routing

Transfers & Conference

Move a call to a colleague, or bring more people in. Transfer targets are agents within your own workspace.

Blind transfer

Sends the customer straight to another agent and drops you from the call. Fastest, with no briefing. Requires Blind Transfer.

Warm / attended transfer

Rings the other agent first so you can brief them, then Complete transfer to hand off. The customer stays on hold music throughout. Requires Warm Transfer.

Add participant

Keeps you on the line and adds another agent or an external number into a conference bridge. Requires Add Participant.

One call log, not two. A transfer or conference doesn't create a separate call record — the original log keeps a Participants column listing everyone who was on the call (original agent, transfer targets, added numbers). Supervisors who listen/whisper/barge never appear there.
If the receiving agent declines or doesn't answer a warm transfer, the customer is reconnected to you — the call isn't dropped.

Voicemail & Recording

Voicemail

When enabled for your role, a declined or unanswered direct call drops to voicemail. Messages appear on the Voicemail tab with a themed player, an unread badge, and the real message length.

Call recording

Recording runs automatically on outbound, inbound-to-agent and toll-free queue calls when your workspace/role allows it. Recordings attach to the matching row in Call Logs and appear on the Recordings page.

Two separate permissions: Play Voicemails lets you hear them; Redirect Declined Calls to Voicemail is what actually lets a call fall through to voicemail instead of a busy tone.
Records

Logs, Recordings & Contacts

Call Logs

Full inbound / outbound / missed history with date, agent and search filters, pagination, a Participants column, and inline recording playback. Tabs split All / Incoming / Outgoing / Missed.

Recordings

A dedicated page listing every recording with the themed audio player. Editable disposition straight from the row.

Contacts

Each contact holds multiple phone numbers and addresses. Create / edit / delete are individually permissioned. Inbound and outbound numbers are matched to a contact's name, company and job title.

Pipeline

Leads & Follow-ups

Leads

Persistent customer records that build up interaction history across many calls — not per-call throwaways. Name, phone, address, latest-disposition, tags and attachments.

Follow-ups dashboard

Totals for All, Today and Missed follow-ups, with a sidebar badge for what's due.

Outbound at scale

Auto Dialer

Campaign-based outbound dialing with disposition capture and reporting.

  1. An admin creates a campaign with a ring strategy and its own disposition set.
  2. Contacts are uploaded as XLSX / CSV and assigned to agents. A contact is locked to one agent so two people never get the same record.
  3. The agent works the campaign from the auto-dial screen — dial, talk, skip if needed, and capture a disposition on each contact.
  4. Progress and outcomes roll into campaign reports, which export.

Toll-Free & IVR

Toll-free numbers with inbound queue campaigns and a data-driven IVR. Available when your workspace has the toll-free feature enabled.

Campaigns

Route a toll-free number into a queue that rings a set of agents, with its own greeting and hold behaviour.

Dynamic IVR

Greetings and digit options are stored in the database — build and change an IVR menu without editing the phone system.

Live dashboard & reports

A real-time toll-free dashboard (who's waiting, how long) plus toll-free reports. The Toll-Free report is only visible to workspaces with the feature enabled.

Supervisor Tools · Permission Required

Supervisor Monitoring

The Live Supervisor page shows active calls across your workspace or your supervised teams. Three modes — switch between them live without dropping the call.

Listen

Silently monitor a live call — neither the agent nor the customer knows. For quality monitoring.

Whisper

Coach the agent privately — the customer can't hear you. For real-time guidance mid-call.

Barge

Join the conversation — both the agent and the customer hear you. For escalations.

ModeAgent hears youCustomer hears you
ListenNoNo
WhisperYesNo
BargeYesYes
Permissions. Each mode is its own permission — Listen to Live Calls, Whisper on Calls, Barge into Calls — on top of Monitor Calls. If you supervise specific teams, the matching per-team privilege (Monitor / Listen / Whisper / Barge team calls) must also be switched on for that team, and the agent must be a member of it.
A monitoring leg is never written to the call logs, and it drops automatically the instant the call it's attached to ends.

Administration

Users & Agents

Create an agent

Add the user, pick a role. A SIP endpoint is provisioned automatically with a workspace-unique username and an extension allocated from your workspace's start number.

Assign a DID

Optionally give the agent a direct number for outbound caller ID. The DID picker lists local numbers only — toll-free numbers route to their campaign queue and can't be assigned to an agent.

Live status

Ready / On-Call / Paused / Wrap-up / Offline, driven by call events in real time and shown on the supervisor and owner dashboards.

Two-factor auth

TOTP 2FA with QR enrolment can be required per account. Stored SIP and TOTP secrets are encrypted with AES-256-GCM.

Analytics

Dashboards for the day-to-day, and a reports hub with PDF / Excel export for everything else.

Agent dashboard — your own KPI row and activity charts. Owner dashboard — team-level rollups with an agent filter.

Inbound and outbound reports with direction, status, duration and date filters. Export to PDF or Excel.

Desktop

Desktop App

The Windows desktop app wraps the same workspace and talks to your hosted ringNex backend over the network.

  1. Download Ringnex-Setup-1.0.0.exe from the homepage and run it. On first run, Windows SmartScreen may warn — choose More info → Run anyway (the build is unsigned).
  2. Launch ringNex and sign in with your workspace, email and password — same credentials as the browser.
  3. Allow microphone access when prompted.
  4. During a call you can pop out the call into an always-on-top window — answer, mute, hold, DTMF, transfer and hang up from it while you work in other apps.
  5. Team Chat push notifications work inside the desktop app too.

Troubleshooting

Calls won't connect

Check your internet, confirm microphone permission is granted for the site, and reload. In the desktop app, fully quit and relaunch.

No audio

Check the output device in your OS. Make sure the browser tab isn't muted.

Inbound calls don't ring

Confirm your status is Ready (not Paused/Offline) and the ringNex tab or app is still open. Your SIP endpoint must be registered.

"This team does not allow the requested monitoring action"

You're a supervisor and the target agent's team hasn't granted your per-team monitoring privilege. Ask an admin to enable Monitor team calls plus Listen / Whisper / Barge team calls for you on that team, and confirm the agent is a member of it.

Recording won't play

The recording may still be finalising just after the call ends — try again in a minute. If it never appears, recording may not be enabled for your role.

A page or button is missing

The menu and call-control buttons follow your role's permissions. Ask a workspace admin to grant the permission described in this guide.

Still stuck? Contact your workspace administrator, or tech@unisoftwares.com.
