Features

Everything your calling team needs

Each capability is built in and switched on per person, so everyone gets exactly the tools their job requires — and nothing they don't.

Built-in phone

Make and take calls right in the app — no separate handset or plugin.

  • Dial by keypad, or straight from a contact, lead or call record
  • Mute, hold with music, and a keypad for menu prompts
  • Caller's name shown automatically from your saved contacts

Call recording

Conversations are captured automatically so you always have the full picture.

  • Outbound, inbound and campaign calls
  • One-click playback from any call record
  • Turned on per person by an administrator

Voicemail

Missed calls don't have to mean missed customers.

  • Callers can leave a message when no one picks up
  • Play messages in the app, with an unread count
  • Enabled per role, so it fits how your team works

Call history & outcomes

A complete, searchable record of every call — with the result attached.

  • Filter by date, agent, direction or keyword
  • Tag each call with an outcome from your own list
  • See everyone who was on the call, even after a transfer

Leads & follow-ups

Keep a running relationship with every customer, not just a call log.

  • One record per customer that grows with every call
  • Notes, tags and attached files in one place
  • Dated follow-ups with a reminder for what's due today

Contacts

Your customer directory, ready to dial.

  • Multiple phone numbers and addresses per contact
  • Call directly from any contact
  • Incoming numbers matched to a name, company and title

Outbound campaigns

Work through a list of numbers quickly and consistently.

  • Upload a spreadsheet and split it across agents
  • Each contact is locked to one agent — no double calls
  • Skip, disposition and move on; results roll into reports

Toll-free lines & menus

Give customers a number to call and guide them to the right place.

  • Route a toll-free number into a queue of agents
  • Build a press-1, press-2 menu — no technical help needed
  • Live view of who's waiting and for how long

Live call monitoring

Supervisors can support agents while a call is happening.

  • Listen silently for quality checks
  • Whisper to coach the agent — the customer can't hear
  • Barge in to join and help directly

Teams

Group agents and give supervisors just the right amount of oversight.

  • Assign supervisors to the teams they manage
  • Limit what each supervisor can see and do, per team
  • Keep reporting and monitoring focused

Agents & access

Add people in minutes and control exactly what they can do.

  • Each agent gets their own extension automatically
  • Assign a direct number for outbound caller ID
  • Secure sign-in with optional two-step verification

Roles & permissions

Decide what every role can see and do — down to individual buttons.

  • Build your own roles from a large set of permissions
  • Menus and call controls adapt to each role automatically
  • One active session per person

Phone numbers

Manage the numbers your team calls from and customers call in on.

  • Keep an inventory of your numbers in one place
  • Assign direct numbers to agents
  • Route inbound calls where they should go

Do-not-call list

Stay compliant without slowing agents down.

  • Import a list or add numbers by hand
  • Calls to listed numbers are blocked automatically
  • Give specific roles an override when needed

Reports & analytics

Know how the team is doing, and prove it.

  • Agent and team dashboards for the day-to-day
  • Agent performance ranked by activity
  • Export any report to PDF or Excel

Team chat

Coordinate with colleagues without leaving the app.

  • Group channels and one-to-one messages
  • Emoji and file sharing
  • Desktop alerts for new messages
On the call

Handle the whole call from one screen

Everything an agent needs during a live call is right there — and in the desktop app it can float in its own always-on-top window while they work elsewhere.

  • Mute, hold, keypad, transfer and add people
  • Bring another agent or an outside number into the call
  • A wrap-up screen opens automatically when the call ends
  • Set the outcome, the next follow-up, notes and files in seconds
